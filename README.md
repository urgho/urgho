### About me

I'm 21, based in São Paulo, and I work as a **Back-end Developer at ICR Assessoria**, stepping into the front end whenever a project calls for it — that's how I built Rouba Bandeira, end to end. >
> **What it gave me as a developer:** prioritizing with incomplete information, deciding under pressure without freezing, communicating clearly in a critical situation, and hitting deadlines with emotional stability. Same posture I bring to a production incident.

**Sales Associate** · Arnold Sports Festival — Official Store · 2026
